Print a `requirement` statement that a comment line after U+2028 or U+2029 cuts,
or that ends such a line after a `style`, `classDef`, or `class` statement, as
written to its end, with a name in quotes kept, so that the file it prints can
be read again: `element`, the character, `%% c`, an empty line, and `"e"` no
longer print `e` for `"e"`.
