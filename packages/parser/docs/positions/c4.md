### Statements

An element, relationship, or update covers its keyword through the closing
parenthesis read at a unit start. A boundary covers its keyword through its
closing brace, including its `beforeBody` blocks and nested statements. An empty
description block covers `accDescr` through `}` in either child list. [fixture:
c4/lex-bare-across-close-and-line] [fixture: c4/db-keyword-relindex] [fixture:
c4/gram-update-empty-string] [fixture: c4/gram-boundary-nested] [fixture:
c4/gram-empty-block-after-args-before-body] [fixture:
c4/gram-empty-block-boundary-between-line-brace] [fixture:
c4/gram-empty-block-header-gap]

A title or accessibility statement covers its keyword through its last token:
the value, colon, or closing brace. Its trailing whitespace is included only
where its value preserves it. [fixture: c4/db-title-whitespace] [fixture:

### C4Literal, C4NamedAttribute, and StringLiteral

Bare arguments, named keys, colon values, and block values cover their text
without exterior whitespace. Internal whitespace, original line terminators, and
parentheses inside a bare argument remain in the range. A null argument, key, or
value has no node and no range. [fixture: c4/lex-bare-across-close-and-line]

`$` and `=`. A string covers both quotes; its value excludes them and its `raw`
keeps the entire source range. Common trivia inside a range stays in the range
